# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and MFA
All employees must use the company SSO with multi-factor authentication. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with production access. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are never shared over chat or email.

## Laptop and device rules
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access email and chat only through the managed mobile profile. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to Security within 1 hour by opening a P1 ticket.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is Restricted: it may only be processed in approved Google Cloud projects in the asia-south1 region, and it must never be pasted into external AI tools.

## Access reviews
Managers review their team's access to production systems every quarter. Access that is not re-approved within 10 working days is revoked automatically.
