# Leave and Travel Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 10 unused days carry over to the next year; anything above that lapses on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Work from home
Teams follow a hybrid model: at least 3 days per week in the office. Managers can approve up to 2 weeks of fully remote work per quarter.

## Travel and expenses
Domestic travel must be booked through the travel desk at least 7 days in advance. Daily meal allowance is INR 1,500 in metro cities and INR 1,000 elsewhere. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with itemised receipts; claims above INR 25,000 need director approval.
